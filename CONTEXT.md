# Hudyat — Domain glossary

Filipino Sign Language (FSL) learning: students practise signs in front of a camera and the app
recognizes them.

## Recognition

- **Prompt**: the sign a student is currently asked to make.
- **Static sign**: a sign recognized from a single handshape (the alphabet letters).
- **Moving sign**: a sign recognized from a movement over time (category signs other than the alphabet).
- **Hold**: keeping a static sign steady until the app accepts it. A hold is *dropped* when the
  sign stops qualifying (hand lost, unsure prediction, too much movement, a different sign) and
  has to start over.
- **Time to recognize**: from the student's hand coming into view on a prompt to the correct sign
  being accepted. The speed target is stated in this measure.
- **False accept**: a wrong sign accepted as correct. Treated as worse than slowness, because it
  teaches the student the wrong sign.
